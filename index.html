The firm

From Scandinavia to the world.

siwa law was founded in Denmark with a global outlook. We are a multilingual boutique firm, committed to getting business done in our clients' best interests.

For more than twelve years we have negotiated and closed deals across six continents. Our ties to Scandinavia, Spain, Brazil, the Middle East and Indonesia give us the cultural insight that local and cross-border work needs.

Entrepreneurship, AI and tech, sport and international business are where we work best. We look at each matter as a whole, so the legal work serves your business goals.

We work remote first: meetings happen online, which saves you time and cost wherever you are.

We listen before we solve. Openness and understanding first. Then we act, fast.

The name

Named after the Siwa Oasis.

Siwa lies in Egypt's Western Desert. It was home to the Oracle of Amun, where Alexander the Great came for guidance. An advisor's role: helping others see the way forward. That is the role we want to play for ambitious clients in a global world.
