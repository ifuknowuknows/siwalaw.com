Privacy policy

Updated 24 November 2026

SIWA Law Advokatanpartsselskab ("SIWA Law") handles personal data of its clients in the course of providing legal assistance and has therefore adopted this privacy policy, which describes how your data is processed and the rights you have as a registered person.

To best protect your personal data, we assess the risk of our data processing negatively affecting your fundamental rights, and we process your personal data based on the following basic principles:

  • Processing is done in a lawful, fair, and transparent manner towards you, and is subject to purpose limitation.
  • Processing is done based on a principle of data minimisation.
  • Processing is done based on a principle of accuracy, to ensure that the data we process about you is correct and up to date.
  • Processing is done based on a principle of storage limitation.
  • Processing is done based on a principle of integrity and confidentiality.

Data controller

SIWA Law is responsible for ensuring that your personal data is processed in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (the General Data Protection Regulation) and relevant national legislation.

Processing of personal data as part of our legal advice

At SIWA Law, we process personal data about you as an unavoidable part of the advice we provide you and in our ongoing contact with you.

Types of personal data

In connection with the provision of legal advice, we process personal data about you, which among other things includes:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Address
  • Telephone number
  • Job function
  • Financial circumstances
  • Wealth
  • Family relations
  • Employer
  • Insurance relationships
  • Previous residence
  • Relationships with third parties
  • Social circumstances

In some cases, we process special categories of personal data (sensitive data) about you, which includes:

  • Racial or ethnic origin
  • Political, religious and philosophical beliefs
  • Trade union membership
  • Genetic or biometric data for the purpose of unique identification
  • Health and sexual matters

Purpose of processing and the legal basis

We collect and store your data for specific and legitimate purposes, such as:

  • When we register you as a client or contact person in our case system for ongoing correspondence and invoicing
  • When we need the information to get an overview of the facts of the case and thus create the best possible basis for assisting and advising you legally
  • When we are obliged to obtain information in accordance with the Anti-Money Laundering Act or other legislation
  • When we have a legitimate interest in processing the data, for example, to prevent fraud or protect our own or others' legal interests
  • When we have obtained your explicit consent for the processing of personal data for a specific purpose

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. If you choose a language, that choice is stored in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and its fonts are delivered by jsDelivr; both receive your IP address as part of delivering the pages. If you email or call us, we use your details only to reply to you and, if you become a client, as described above.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access your personal data, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have data erased or its processing restricted, to object to processing, and to data portability, within the limits set by law. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise your rights, contact us at info@siwalaw.com.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), www.datatilsynet.dk.
